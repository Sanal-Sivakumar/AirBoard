# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who regularly move text between personal computers, phones, and tablets on the same local network and do not want clipboard contents routed through a cloud service. Their primary workflow is pairing devices once, leaving synchronization enabled, and trusting that a copy on one approved device becomes available on the others without extra interaction.

## Product Purpose

AirBoard provides direct, local-network clipboard synchronization between explicitly paired devices. Success means fast and predictable text synchronization, mutual device authentication, honest platform behavior, understandable recovery when a device is unavailable, and no clipboard plaintext leaving the user's approved peer set.

## Brand Personality

Calm, secure, and precise. The interface should communicate quiet confidence and make connection, pairing, and encryption state understandable without exposing users to protocol jargon during routine use.

## Anti-references

Avoid security theater, unverifiable claims, decorative complexity, cloud-dashboard conventions, alarming permission prompts without context, and visual redesigns that obscure the familiar copy-pair-sync workflow.

## Design Principles

- Make trust explicit: clearly distinguish discovered, pairing, trusted, connected, and unavailable devices.
- Practice honest security: only label a session secure after mutual authentication and encrypted transport are established.
- Keep synchronization ambient: normal clipboard movement should require no repeated interaction.
- Explain exceptional states: permissions, platform limits, and recovery actions should be specific and actionable.
- Preserve user control: pairing, unpairing, pause, and clipboard-history behavior remain visible and reversible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and interaction behavior. Do not rely on color alone for connection or security state, support keyboard and screen-reader use, respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain usable layouts at narrow widths and high text scale, and keep security fingerprints selectable and readable.
